FAQ

What's actually downloading?

Transmission moves the bytes and Jackett aggregates your indexers. Fonte is the part that decides what to fetch and where it goes.

What if it isn't out yet?

It goes on the watchlist, which checks your indexers on an interval and grabs a release when one turns up at the quality you asked for.

Can I automate it?

Automations are rules: pick when they fire, when a torrent finishes or errors or stalls, when the watchlist finds a match, or on a schedule, then write what the agent should do about it in a sentence.

Do I have to talk to it?

No. Click a magnet link or open a .torrent and it lands in Fonte like any other client. The chat is there for when describing what you want is faster than going to find it.

Can I use it from my phone?

It talks over WhatsApp, so you can ask from anywhere and it answers in the thread. Pairing is a QR scan, like WhatsApp Web.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac. The installer brings the rest.

Is it free?

Yes, open source and free to use.
